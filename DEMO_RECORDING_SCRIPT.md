### 💡 Pro-Tips for Recording
1. **Pacing:** Speak at a steady, natural pace. Pause for half a second after each question so judges hear the assistant reply.
2. **Audio:** Use your regular headset or microphone in a quiet room.
3. **If You Stumble:** Don't restart from the beginning! Just pause for 2 seconds and re-say that sentence; you can easily trim the pause in any video editor.
